Transparency & Honesty

Understanding how affiliate links support SauceGiver.

Last Updated: November 27, 2026

At SauceGiver, we believe in transparency and honesty with our readers. This Affiliate Disclosure explains how affiliate links work on our website and how we may earn revenue when you interact with certain product links.

01 What Are Affiliate Links?

Some of the products featured on our website, including fashion outfits, nightwear, clothing, footwear, bags, jewellery, and other accessories, may contain affiliate links.

Affiliate links are special tracking links that allow us to earn a commission when visitors click on those links and make qualifying purchases through participating retailers.

This means that when you purchase a product through certain links on our website, we may receive a small commission from the retailer at no additional cost to you.

02 How We Earn Commissions

Our website showcases fashion products from various third-party retailers and online shopping platforms.

Some of these retailers operate affiliate marketing programmes. When you visit their websites through our affiliate links and complete an eligible purchase, we may earn a commission.

However, not every product or link featured on our website is an affiliate link. Some links may direct you to third-party websites without generating any commission for us.

03 No Additional Cost to You

Purchasing through an affiliate link does not increase the price you pay for a product.

The commission, if applicable, is paid by the retailer or affiliate programme, not added to your purchase price.

Product prices, shipping charges, taxes, discounts, and other purchasing conditions are determined by the respective retailer.

04 Product Information and Availability

We showcase products from different retailers to help visitors discover fashion inspiration, clothing styles, nightwear, and accessories.

Product descriptions, images, prices, sizes, availability, shipping information, and return policies may change over time.

We encourage visitors to verify all relevant product details directly on the retailer's website before making a purchase.

We are not responsible for changes made by third-party retailers after products are featured on our website.

05 Our Editorial Independence

Our goal is to showcase fashion products and help visitors discover styles that match their interests.

The presence of an affiliate link does not automatically mean that a product is the best choice or that we personally endorse every aspect of it.

We aim to present products and recommendations honestly. Affiliate commissions do not guarantee positive coverage or favourable placement.

06 Third-Party Websites

When you click a product link on our website, you may be redirected to an external retailer or shopping platform.

These third-party websites operate independently and have their own privacy policies, terms of service, payment procedures, shipping policies, and return conditions.

We do not control their websites, transactions, products, or customer service.

Please review the applicable policies of the retailer before completing a purchase.

07 Your Support

When you purchase through our affiliate links, you help support the operation and development of this website.

Your support allows us to continue discovering fashion products, publishing content, and improving the experience we provide to our visitors.

We genuinely appreciate your trust and support.
